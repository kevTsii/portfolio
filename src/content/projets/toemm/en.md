SaaS platform that lets retailers run their business online: storefront page, product catalog, price lists, inventory, orders, invoicing and payouts. A supervision back office lets the platform team steer everything, from category setup to customer support.

A Laravel REST API carries the business logic and is consumed by a Vue 3 admin interface. The code follows a strict Clean Architecture, split by business domain across each of the four layers, with access partitioned by user profile.

Project co-owner, 6 months.
