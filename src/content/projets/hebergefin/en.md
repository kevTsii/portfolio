Financial management application for a housing non-profit, dedicated to tracking the loans granted to residents and their repayments.

The application centralizes outflows (loans) and inflows (repayments) to give the organization a clear view of the amounts committed and the balances still owed.

Internal application, accessible only through a VPN.
