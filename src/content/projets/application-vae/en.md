Business application that runs Validation of Prior Learning (VAE) journeys, from the first sales contact to invoicing. Tracking of beneficiaries and their files (Livret 1, Livret 2, statuses, history), management of consultants and their assignments, invoicing, reporting and forecasting.

A REST API ingests leads from external platforms and assigns them automatically to sales reps based on workload and availability rules. The application is organized into Symfony bundles by domain, with role-based permissions.

Contract engagement, 14 months, working directly with the client. Sector: training and professional coaching.
