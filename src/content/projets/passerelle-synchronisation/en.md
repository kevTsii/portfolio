Middleware application consuming the APIs of two tracking platforms to compare their data, detect discrepancies and synchronize information in both directions.

The core challenge: two third-party systems, two formats, no control over how they evolve. The hard part is not calling an API, it is handling desynchronizations, partial failures and contract changes on the vendor side without corrupting data.

Two-year engagement, under contract and working directly with the client. Sector: outsourcing services. Internal application, not publicly accessible.
