B2B2C platform connecting the three players of France's CPF training scheme: training providers, certification bodies and trainees. Catalog of certifications and accreditations, trainee enrollment and follow-up through to the admission result, invoicing and payment by card or SEPA direct debit.

Integration with third-party certification platforms and synchronization with EDOF, separate back offices for each type of player, generation of certificates and invoices, and scheduled jobs for renewals and batch payments.

Contract engagement, 14 months, working directly with the client. Sector: professional training.
